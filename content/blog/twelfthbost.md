This week on elemental dungeon we had a lot of work to do based on the playtest from the previous week. We were able to get a lot of great feedback and were able to sort through the most important tasks that we needed to complete and prioritize those for the upcoming playtests and the end of the semester coming up.

My tasks this week were to fix a bug with the fullscreen of the game. Whenever we had it in fullscreen the tileset and UI would pin to the upper left corner and the UI would take up too much of the screen. This in particular did not take too much time to fix and was pretty simple once I was able to pinpoint was exactly the issue was.

My new task for the week was to add visual effects to the wizard attacks based on their typing. Now there is a damage counter and a super effective/not very effective pop ups to add some visual flair to the game. This was much needed based on feedback from the playtest and our own thoughts on how the game felt. These tasks took a while to do I estimated about 2 hours and spent around 2.5 but some of that time going to debugging and looking around at documentation.
