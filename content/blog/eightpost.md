For this week of elemental dungeon there are a lot of changes and new ideas that we have decided on and my responsibility is creating a procedurely generated maze for the players to explore in between levels. Good thing for us this is a mostly solved problem and Godot and there are a lot of already coded solutions and the main responsibility I am going to have is being able to modify and make the code work within our project.

I am using a backtracking recursive approach to the maze to ensure the maze is always completable. My estimate for time on this feature is about 4-6 hours of work for the basic implementation. So far I have spent around 4 hours and still have not been able to figure it fully out yet but I have made good progress in adding it. Later on we will want to add elemental puzzles to ensure that the players can use their elements and work together to solve puzzles.
