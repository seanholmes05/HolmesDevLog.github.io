For this week of elemental dungeon there are a lot of changes and new ideas that we have decided on and my responsibility is creating a procedurely generated maze for the players to explore in between levels. I was able to finish fully implementing that this week and start working on the new UI elements for the new arena.

I also worked on redoing the arena for redoing first boss and tutorial with a new asset pack. I also have put in the new 4 elemental wizards to replace the older characters.

This week I estimated about 2 hours for the maze to be finished being implemented, 1 hr for the new UI, and 2 hours for the elemental wizards. In total it took about 8 hours total so it was a lot more time than I originally anticipated.
