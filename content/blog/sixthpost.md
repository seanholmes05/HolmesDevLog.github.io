This week was another full week of development for Elemental Dungeon. We had a lot to get done and also had our first full playtest in which we got a lot of really good feedback which we will use to continue to make our game. There was a lot of feedback on it not being satisfying enough and players wanting more of a fast pace and satisfying experience so that is what we will try and focus on as the weeks continue. Our goal for the next week is to get a lot more of the simple controls working with the UI and having more connected screens.

This week I worked on getting the players stat menu to work with 3 points that were allocated to the players whenever the defeat one of each of the bosses.

## Playtest 1

The playtest consisted of everything we had gotten done during milestone one. We had a tutorial boss and one main boss and a preliminary elemental combo attack.

We got a lot of good feedback from the combo attack so we are going to be making a lot of changes to that and continue to try and make it better

## Blockers

We are currently running into a lot more blockers as we continue development especially on the art side. We need new character sprites, background and arena art, animations and a few other smaller assets that take a lot of time to develop for us or to find in general.
