This week was a lot of ideation and trying to narrow scope and nail down the elemental combo attack and lock in the defence stat for the player stat menu. There are a few key things this week that were talked about like a boss redesign for floor 1 and what exactly the players will be doing in between bosses.

This week I was responsible for the defence implementation portion of the development. I needed to be able to figure out the best way to implement the added defence but will need further playtesting for balancing with the harder bosses later.

## Blockers

We are still currently running into a lot more blockers as we continue development especially on the art side. We need new character sprites, background and arena art, animations and a few other smaller assets that take a lot of time to develop for us or to find in general.

We need to research more sprites for new bosses and tilesets for the arenas
